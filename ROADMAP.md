# Roadmap

SaaSKit Lite is intentionally small at `v1.0.0`. The goal is to keep the foundation understandable while leaving room for real product growth.

## Near Term

- Multi-tenancy support for organizations and workspaces
- Expanded role and permission model beyond `admin` and `user`
- Password reset and email verification flows
- Docker-based local development
- CI workflows for tests, linting, and release checks

## Mid Term

- Billing and subscription management integrations
- Team invitations and workspace membership flows
- Audit logs and activity history
- File upload module with signed URLs
- OpenAPI or Swagger generation for the API

## Longer Term

- Full web admin panel starter
- More granular role management and permission policies
- Webhooks module with retry and delivery logs
- Mobile improvements including secure token storage and polished UI flows
- Additional starter modules such as notifications, analytics, and account settings
